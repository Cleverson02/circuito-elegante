Check-in/Check-out:
- "O check in é a partir das 14h e o check out até o meio dia" → check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00

Breakfast:
- "Café da manhã está incluso na diária e é servido das 7:30 as 10:30 da manhã" → 7:30–10:30
- Non-guests: R$96 + 10% service

WiFi:
- "Oferecemos internet wi-fi para uso dos clientes" — may have local infrastructure interruptions

Payment Methods:
- Major credit cards, national bank transfers (at booking only), cash.
- NOT accepted: traveler's checks, personal checks, international transfers.

Pet Policy:
- Guests must notify property in advance, analysis + confirmation, pet hosting policy signed at booking if approved.
- → pet-friendly conditional (not a blanket yes)

Parking:
- "Não cobramos o estacionamento dentro da Pousada do Ouro mas temos um número de vagas limitadas" → FREE, limited garage spaces.
- Street parking when full; external paid parking can be recommended.

Children:
- Under 6 with parents stay free in suites only (docs required).
- Extra beds in suites for 6+ at additional rate; cribs on request.

Cancellation:
- Special periods (New Year, Carnival, FLIP): 100% pre-payment, no refunds. >14 days: credit voucher (120 days). <14 days: no refund.
- Regular: 50% pre-payment. >7 days: credit voucher (90 days). <7 days: no refund/credit (50% penalty).

NOT FOUND: pool heated status, smoking policy, accessibility details.
