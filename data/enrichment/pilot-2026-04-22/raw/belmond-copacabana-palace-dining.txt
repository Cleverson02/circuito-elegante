PÉRGULA:
- Cuisine: Carioca/Brazilian (also described as Mediterranean-Brazilian fusion on about page)
- Description: "Soak-up the colourful spirit of Rio from one of the city's most celebrated Carioca restaurants."
- Location: Poolside seating available with Copacabana Beach views
- Hours:
  * Breakfast: 6:30am–10:30am (Mon–Fri); 7am–11am (Sat–Sun)
  * Lunch: noon–4pm (Mon–Fri)
  * Brunch: 1pm–4pm (Sundays)
  * Dinner: 6pm–midnight (kitchen closes 11pm)
- Specialties: Feijoada (Saturday buffet, 1–4pm); Sunday brunch with sparkling wine
- Menus: Lunch/dinner, pizza, wine list, drinks
- Booking: SevenRooms platform

MEE RESTAURANT:
- Cuisine: Pan-Asian
- Chef: Alberto Morisawa
- Accolades: Only Michelin-starred pan-Asian restaurant in Rio
- Description: "Pair exotic specialty dishes with one of 60 imported sakes"
- Hours: Open daily from 7pm
- Specialties: Omakase experience (private sushi bar dining); seasonal creations
- Menus: Dinner menu, sake and wine list, drink menu
- Pricing: Omakase R$950 + 10% service per person
- Booking: SevenRooms platform

POOL BAR:
- Cuisine: Light fare
- Description: Handmade pizzas and light bites
- Hours: Seasonal; currently 11am–6pm (Fri–Sun)

GRILL DO PÉRGULA:
- Cuisine: Brazilian grilled meats
- Description: "Prime cuts of Brazilian meat served with a selection of sauces and garnishes"
- Hours: Mon–Thu, 7:30pm–9:30pm
- Pricing: R$445 + 10% service (excluding beverages); max 8 guests per group
