=== Accommodations ===
Suíte Duplex: 60 m². Family (couple + up to 2 children <12). Master bedroom on mezzanine, sofa bed on ground floor. King bed, AC, TV, WiFi, fireplace, jacuzzi, minibar.
Suíte Deluxe: 48 m². Couple. Fireplace near bed, private deck with jacuzzi bathtub. Queen bed, AC, TV, WiFi, minibar. Ideal for weekend getaways, remote work.

=== Dining & Gastronomy ===
NÓR Sensi Restaurant: unique lunches and dinners, contemporary sophisticated cuisine, open to public. Digital menu, wine list, WhatsApp reservations.
Adega (Wine Cellar): mirrored glass cube overlooking pool. International + Brazilian wine selection. Wine Emotion system for by-the-glass. Intimate dinners and tastings.
Pool Bar: informal dining around heated pool. Cocktails, wines, portions, sandwiches, salads, desserts. Nature views.
Room Service: breakfast, lunch, dinner in suites. Restaurant/bar menu options.
Breakfast: served in NÓR Sensi or via Room Service. Accommodates dietary restrictions. Mostly locally-sourced ingredients prepared on-site.
