SOURCE: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copacabana_Palace
FETCHED: 2026-04-19

OPENING YEAR: August 13, 1923
ARCHITECT: Joseph Gire (French), "drew inspiration from two famous hotels on the French Riviera"

TOTAL ROOMS (historical/full): 243 total — 116 apartments and 127 suites across main and annex buildings
NOTE: Current operational count per official site is 140 (annex under renovation until Oct 2026)

NOTABLE GUESTS: Walt Disney, Marlene Dietrich, Josephine Baker, Princess Diana, Paul McCartney, Madonna, Lady Gaga

HISTORICAL BACKGROUND:
- Built 1919-1923 by Octavio Guinle and Francisco Castro Silva
- Initiated by President Epitacio Pessoa to accommodate visitors for the Independence Centenary International Exposition
- Construction delays due to imported materials and environmental conditions

NOTABLE EVENTS:
- 1928: President Washington Luis shot at the hotel
- Featured a casino until President Eurico Gaspar Dutra banned gambling in 1946

CURRENT STATUS:
- Operating as "Copacabana Palace, A Belmond Hotel"
- "voted the best hotel in South America multiple times, including in 2009, when it won the World Travel Award"
- LVMH acquired the property in 2018

ARCHITECTURAL STYLE:
- Drew from French Riviera luxury hotel aesthetics
- Carrara marble and Bohemian crystal details
- Art-deco
